# TableWise — Product Definition

> Source of truth for what the product is. If code or another doc disagrees with this file, raise it; do not silently pick one.
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-23.

## What it is

TableWise is a weekly meal planner for households. A person picks dinners for the week, gets one grocery list, cooks with a step-by-step mode, and keeps a private record of what they made. Home Tasks, a short private to-do list for the house, sits beside the meal loop (added by the product owner, 2026-11-17).

It is **not** a recipe social network, a recipe scraper, or a general AI chat.

## Who it is for

A person who plans and cooks for a household: several eaters, allergies and dislikes, a fixed set of kitchen devices, a budget, and limited time on weeknights.

## Stage and hypothesis

- **Stage:** gated pilot. Optional email and password accounts (guests need none), no payments, no marketing site.
- **Hypothesis under test:** people find the plan → groceries → cook loop valuable enough to come back and plan another week.
- **Measure of success:** `next_week_plan_created` following a `plan_completed` for the same person.

## Core loop

1. **Plan** (`/meals`) — the Week as a cooking plan: one dated week (Monday–Sunday) grouped by cooking day, each dish shown once on the day it is cooked with how many servings to make and the dates it serves, opened as a draft and approved in one action (product owner, 2026-11-20, replacing the 21 Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner slots of 2026-11-19; see Cooking plan below). Screen: [docs/cooking-plan.md](cooking-plan.md). Previous and next week controls open other weeks. "Browse recipes" (`/meals?browse`) searches and filters the catalog and keeps up to 12 picks. The recipe browser is the only place to set recipe filters: Cooking level, Total time (Any or 15–60 min, ≤30 min by default), Dietary (with More dietary options), Kid favourites, Kitchen tools (your devices and No-cook), Source, and More filters (protein, meal type, must include, must exclude, cuisine, My picks with minimum rating, With my notes, Modified by me; restored by the product owner, 2026-11-19). Food deals above the results shows sample offers by food category; Show recipes filters to recipes using that ingredient (product owner, 2026-11-18). Rules: [docs/week-plan-filters.md](week-plan-filters.md).
2. **Shop** (`/grocery-list`) — one list built from the approved dishes of the Week, each counted once and scaled from its recipe's original yield to its "Make X servings" (a draft dish, or a recipe only picked in the browser, adds nothing until Approve plan or Save changes; 2026-11-20), grouped by category, with pantry items marked. Share or export it. Add household products using translated suggestions or a custom name, with an optional private reference picture. Add products individually or paste a list, review estimated category costs (each category's "$"), and record full or partial purchases. Under the header, "Walk-in estimate" is the sum of every remaining item's price, whatever the filter or search shows, and "Find an item…" searches every category, collapsed ones too (2026-11-20). Removing a main protein can also remove its dishes from the Week. Check the impact before confirming and use Undo to restore removed dishes. Purchased quantities stay available under Checked items. Proteins on hand confirmed for the Week (Cook with what I have) are subtracted once from the rows they match when the amount is known in the same unit (g with kg, ml with L, counts with counts); otherwise the row keeps its whole amount and asks "Do you have enough?" with "Yes, I have enough" (covers that amount) and "Enter what I have"; if the dishes later need more than was confirmed, it asks again about the rest. Manual items are never touched (2026-11-22).
3. **Cook** (`/recipe/:id`) — scale servings, check off ingredients (a checked ingredient is struck through and moves below the unchecked ones, recipe order kept in both; unchecking puts it back; the same in cooking mode's ingredient list, with ＋ and ✓, and Groceries' Checked items are struck through; 2026-11-23), run cooking mode with per-step timers. Ingredients and Instructions fold under their headings (open by default), sliding open and closed over 0.7 s (product owner, 2026-11-21; the second exception to "no decorative animation" after the Weekly deals dot; instant under reduced motion). Finishing cooking mode (Finish, or Exit on the last step) folds both and returns to the recipe with "Made it?" in the middle of the screen and "I made it! ✓" focused (2026-11-21). Start cooking opens Zen mode at step 1 by default. In Zen mode, the ingredients the current step uses (with that step's quantities) are listed below its instruction (moved from above by the product owner, 2026-11-23; the step, its time and timer come first), and they change with Next and Back; each has ＋ to tick it off (✓, struck through, below the others, a second tap restores it; per step, 2026-11-23). A step that uses none shows no ingredient section. A recipe without step-level ingredient data shows "View recipe ingredients" (the full list) instead; ingredients are never guessed (product owner, 2026-11-18).
4. **Remember** (My Cooking Experience on the recipe page) — "I made it", rating, notes, photos.

**Home** (`/`) is the landing page: Today from the Week (or example dishes when nothing is planned today), "Suggest a recipe" (opens Find my dish) and Home Tasks, nothing else. Today is split into Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner, as in the Week (product owner, 2026-11-20); each meal lists the dishes cooked today ("Make X servings") and dishes cooked earlier that are eaten today ("Cooked Sun"), each opening the recipe at that batch and with "Remove dish" (the whole dish, as on the Week, with Undo; 2026-11-20), and its own "+ Add breakfast", "+ Add lunch" or "+ Add dinner". A meal with nothing today is collapsed to its name; opening it shows its "+ Add" (2026-11-20). Only the meal of this time of day is open (6:00–11:00 Breakfast, 11:00–16:00 Lunch, 16:00–23:00 Dinner, local time; from 23:00 to 6:00 none is; product owner, 2026-11-22); every other meal that has dishes is collapsed too, and its header shows each dish on one line: the name (cut short if long), the first two Recipe View nutrients and an underlined "…" that shows all of them below (as on the recipe card, smaller and without the "Est. per serving" heading); opening it shows the cards instead (2026-11-20). Home Tasks starts collapsed, with the number of open tasks beside its title; opening it lists every open task and "View all tasks". Today's dishes are full recipe cards (on wider screens with the photo on the left), so Home can scroll down (product owner, 2026-11-20; it did not scroll before). "+ Add breakfast/lunch/dinner" opens the meal generator (a bottom sheet on a phone, a dialog on desktop; product owner, 2026-11-19): the kind of dish to search (Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner; that meal first, and the dish is saved under the kind chosen), an optional free search ("What would you like for dinner?"), Time (Any time, ≤ 15 min, ≤ 30 min of total time; unknown time never passes), Cooking style (Quick & easy by default) and the household line (servings, restrictions, budget) with Edit for servings, the four marked diets and kitchen equipment, for this dish only (equipment added 2026-11-20). "Generate dinner" shows one catalog recipe labelled "Suggested recipe" with time, servings, scaled ingredients and steps; "Try another", "Edit request" and "Choose from my recipes" (Liked and Imported by me) keep the inputs. Only "Add to today" changes the Week: a new dish cooked today, beside any already there, at the household's adults and kids (or the servings edited in the sheet). Home then shows "Added to today" with Undo, which removes only that dish and only while it is still that recipe on that date. Allergies, devices (as edited for this dish) and meal rules always apply through `checkEligibility`; Week → Plan filters are not applied. No AI is used (`client/src/lib/meal-generator.ts`). When nothing is cooked or eaten today (the same Today), Home shows up to three example cards in place of the meals, in this order: a beef, a chicken and a plant-based main dish (vegetarian or vegan, built around a plant protein first, else vegan-tagged), each a real catalog recipe with its own photo, name and cooking time, labelled "Example", and within the household's requirements; a dish the requirements rule out is replaced by another eligible one, and fewer show when there are not enough; "+ Add breakfast/lunch/dinner" stay below them. Any dish today replaces all of them; removing the last brings them back (product owner, 2026-11-22). **Find my dish** (2026-11-22; the latest plan, 2026-11-23, opens it from Home's "Suggest a recipe", always on Home; "Cook with what I have" was removed from Home the same day; screen 5's "Cook now" opens it too) never sends anyone to the setup: it opens a sheet (a bottom sheet on a phone) already showing one eligible main dish (a lunch or dinner, in Cook now's order; 2026-11-23), so one tap from Home shows a dish and a second starts cooking; "Edit search" shows "What protein do you have?", one editable dropdown, closed at first, placeholder "Select or type a protein…"; opened, it lists Beef, Chicken, Eggs, Fish, Pork, Tofu, Turkey, Beans, Lentils, Chickpeas, Shellfish, Lamb, Cheese, Yogurt, Duck, Goat, Game, Tempeh, Edamame, Split peas, Seitan, Nuts, Seeds, Plant-based meat alternatives; typing narrows them and adds specific kinds (Salmon, Cod, Tuna, Shrimp, Chicken breast, Chicken thighs, Ground beef, Steak, Ground turkey, Bacon); Enter or "Use “…”" takes the person's own text. "+ Leave out an ingredient" opens an optional field (suggestions or own words) adding removable ingredients; "Dietary needs (optional)" adds diets and allergies for this search only, on top of the household's (which stay); a disabled "Upload your fridge · Coming soon" button opens nothing and asks for no permission. "Find my dish" shows one eligible main dish (a lunch or dinner, or a breakfast for Eggs), whose main ingredient is the protein entered, if any, at the specificity entered (Fish matches salmon, Salmon only salmon; chicken broth is not chicken), using none of the Leave out ingredients: its card, "You have …" (the protein and every ingredient tapped as had, without quantities), "You'll also need" (its other ingredients, every one when no protein was entered; "You have everything for this dish" when all are tapped), each row a toggle: "Tap + if you have it. We'll use your picks for the next dish." A tap marks the row ✓ and struck through and moves it below the unchecked rows (recipe order within both); a second tap restores it. A tap records the ingredient by name (`ingredientType`: "1 onion" and "2 onions" are one), not a quantity, for this sheet only, so a later dish already shows it checked; Edit search lists the tapped ingredients as removable chips. Then "Start cooking" (Zen mode at step 1), "Try another" (among dishes of this search not yet shown: the most distinct tapped ingredients, then the fewest other ingredients, then the usual order; when none is left it says so and keeps the dish) and "Edit search". No match: "No matching dish yet. Try another protein or edit your ingredients.", with the inputs kept, and "Edit kitchen" when My kitchen was answered. Rules: `checkEligibility` with the household's requirements; a kitchen never answered filters nothing here, a saved one (Microwave only, No cooking appliances) applies as saved (`client/src/lib/find-my-dish.ts`). Closing the sheet or a reload starts blank (a new search keeps the tapped ingredients); it writes nothing (no Week, groceries, settings or proteins on hand).

**Sign in** (first visit, at any page except a share link): email and password, or "Continue as guest". There is no separate sign-up: the first sign-in with a new email creates the account with the password typed, and the guest's data becomes the account's. Basic Settings shows who is signed in, with Sign out (product owner, 2026-11-18). A signed-in account can add an optional name and photo there; the photo (or the first letter of the name or email) replaces the Settings icon in the header (2026-11-18).

**Your household** (`/household`, guided setup, product owner 2026-11-21, replacing the one-screen form of 2026-11-19): after Sign in or "Continue as guest", a new household answers four questions, one at a time, with "Step N of 4" and Back: (1) cooking style (Quick & easy, Everyday cooking, Chef challenge; a choice advances, "Use everyday cooking" takes the default); (2) "Who are you cooking for?", Adults and Children counters (at least one person, at most 12), with a preview of 1 serving per adult and ½ per child (2 adults and 3 children: 3½ servings); the counters add household members or remove the last one of that kind without food details of their own (a name alone does not count; product owner, 2026-11-21), so diets, allergies, likes and portions from Basic Settings are never deleted and Settings is linked for those people, and a fixed number from Basic Settings stays until the counts are changed; with at least one child, a "Kid-friendly meals" switch (off by default) sits right below Children: lunch and dinner suggestions in Cook now and the Week (draft, Swap, Try another, + Add) rank recipes tagged Kid friendly first, a ranking and never a restriction, so other eligible recipes still appear when none fits; the switch keeps its value when Children goes back to 0 (product owner, 2026-11-21; with no children it is in Personalize my recipes, the same saved preference); (3) "Family food preferences" (renamed from "Family food requirements", product owner, 2026-11-22): "No requirements", "Choose a diet" (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Paleo, Carnivore: the diets the catalog marks explicitly) and "Add allergies or exclusions" (all ten allergies, Gluten and Milk also needing the catalog mark, plus the person's own ingredients to avoid); diets and exclusions can be combined and apply to everyone eating, No requirements clears the others and choosing one clears No requirements, and Continue needs an answer; (4) "My kitchen": Microwave, Air fryer, Stovetop, Oven, or "No cooking appliances" (any number of appliances; that order, and Microwave shown selected in a new setup and saved by Continue, never over a saved answer: product owner, 2026-11-22) (only recipes reviewed as no-cook); unselecting the last appliance means unanswered, not none. Each answer is saved as it is chosen, so Back and a reload keep it and the setup reopens at the question the person reached. Screen 5, "What would you like to do?", appears once the server has the answers (if saving fails the answers stay with Try again): "Cook now", "Plan my week" (`/meals?plan`) and a quieter "Personalize my recipes". Finishing the setup creates no week. Launch always goes Home, a new household too (product owner, 2026-11-22; before, a new or unfinished setup with an empty Week landed here). It stays reachable from the Week and from the TableWise name in the header. A suggestion that needs a missing answer (household size, requirements, or a kitchen never answered) asks only that question (`/household?ask=…`) and then resumes: Cook now, the Week (plan and recipe browser) and Home's "+ Add". A Week that already has dishes is not changed by new answers: when answers that change dishes (Your household, Basic Settings or the Week's cooking days, including the recipe filters shared with the recipe browser; not name, stores or Recipe View) were saved after the Week was planned, the Week asks once "Your preferences have been changed": "Update the week" replaces this week's dishes with a new draft (an approved Week keeps its groceries until Save changes), "Keep my week" leaves it (product owner, 2026-11-20).

**Personalize my recipes** (`/personalize?from=…`, 2026-11-21): one editor opened from screen 5, Cook now ("Personalize"), the Week (under the days) and Settings (top). A plain-language summary at the top follows the draft ("Only Vegetarian recipes. No Sesame. Asian dishes first. Kid-friendly lunches and dinners first."). Three groups: "Family food preferences" (the same controls as the setup; hard rules), "Favorite cuisines" (ranking only: Asian, which stands for Chinese, Indian, Japanese, Korean, Thai and Vietnamese, then every catalog cuisine; overlapping choices count once, and other recipes still appear) and "Cooking preferences (optional)" (Kid-friendly meals and the Mon–Fri time limits; favorites, dislikes, cooking times and weekday or weekend routines stay in Settings, linked). It edits a draft: Save writes only these fields onto the latest settings and returns to where it was opened (from Cook now it picks a fresh suggestion); Cancel returns with nothing changed. On the Week, saved changes never replace dishes by themselves: "Your preferences have been changed" asks first (an approved week keeps its groceries until Save changes), and a dish that no longer meets the requirements shows "No longer fits your preferences: …" on its card.

**Cook now** (2026-11-21): screen 5's "Cook now" opens the Find my dish sheet, the same as Home's "Suggest a recipe" (product owner, 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-23; see Home). Before, it asked for several ticked proteins and opened one recipe on its page with "Suggested for you", "Show another", "Personalize" and a temporary time limit (`/recipe/:id?suggest`); that page still works from an existing link and after Personalize's Save, but no button opens it now.

**Cooking plan** (`/meals?plan`, "Weekly cooking plan", product owner 2026-11-20; full rules in [docs/cooking-plan.md](cooking-plan.md)): seven dated days, Monday to Sunday, today highlighted, grouped by cooking day (redesigned the same day). A cooking day folds under its date (the date is the toggle; folded, it lists the day's dish names on one line, cut short when long). In the current week only today starts open; other weeks start with every day open; adding a dish to a day or moving a day there opens it (product owner, 2026-11-21). A day can hold any number of dishes; two dishes on Wednesday are one cooking day. A scheduled cooking day (a household cooking weekday, or any day with a dish) shows a row for each meal enabled under Basic Settings → Meals to plan, in Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner order, even with no dish yet; a meal with dishes keeps its row if the setting changes; an empty row is "+ Add breakfast", "+ Add lunch" or "+ Add dinner", which opens that meal's picker directly (2026-11-21). A cooking day has "Move day" beside the date and one "+ Add dish" below, which asks Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner and opens that meal's picker (its recipes first); a day without cooking is one line, the date and "+ Add dish". Each dish appears once, on the day it is cooked, under a small label with its meal (Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner), as a compact card (photo on the left) when the day has one or two dishes or a card with the photo on top, in a grid, when it has three or more (product owner, 2026-11-21): the recipe name (opens the recipe at this batch; Back returns to the same week), then one line "45 min · 7 servings · For Mon–Tue" (total time only; the servings label opens Edit servings; the days covered are left out when the dish is eaten only on its cooking day, "Mon–Wed" for consecutive days, "Mon & Wed" for separate ones), then three icon buttons, Swap (arrows, opens the picker), Try another (circular arrows: one tap puts the next eligible recipe for that day and meal that is not already in the Week on the dish, keeping its dates and portions, with Undo; product owner, 2026-11-21) Move to dinner on a lunch dish or Move to lunch on a dinner dish (one tap, same cooking day, recipe, servings, dates and status kept, even when that meal is not enabled; 2026-11-21) and Remove dish (bin), labelled for screen readers (product owner, 2026-11-20). A dish keeps its meal through Swap, Edit servings and moves; dishes saved without one are dinners. Servings for a date are adults + kids × 0.5 (a fixed number from Your household or Basic Settings counts as adults and wins over the household members, 2026-11-21; otherwise the members; with neither, the recipe's own yield), or a direct amount in half servings; Make X servings is their sum, and ingredients scale from the recipe's original yield (asked for when the recipe has none). Edit servings picks any dates from the cooking day to the end of next week, per date adults and kids or a direct amount, with a live total; at least one date, a positive total, no date before the cooking day. Swap keeps the dates and portions. Move day moves all dishes of that day together within the week; portions that would come before the new day are listed and must each be moved to the new day or removed before Move; Cancel changes nothing. Before the first draft, the Week asks once for the cooking days ("Your cooking days": evenings per week, 1–7 or "Not sure yet" = three; optional fixed evenings; the chef challenge day for Chef challenge; the Mon–Fri time limits); fixed evenings beyond the count must be resolved first, by "Cook N evenings" or by unselecting days, and "Plan my week" saves them for later weeks; "Cooking days" under the days changes them (2026-11-21). Before an empty week's draft (after "Your cooking days" when they are asked), the Week asks "Which proteins do you still have?" (Cook with what I have, 2026-11-22): proteins bought last week, unticked, "Add another protein", optional amounts and Minimize groceries; "Use selected proteins" stores them on the Week and drafts with them, "Skip" drafts as usual; with no recorded purchases it offers manual entry or Skip. The draft is made once per week: every fixed evening is a cooking day, then days are added until the weekly count is met, each the farthest from the nearest cooking day (Sunday and Monday adjacent, ties to the earlier day), one eligible dish each, under Dinner if enabled in Meals to plan, else Lunch, else Breakfast (2026-11-21). Reloads keep every edit. "Approve plan", in the header beside the status with "Approval builds your grocery list.", copies the dishes to Groceries; afterwards the header shows "View groceries", and after any edit "Save changes". An always-open "Weekly deals · N offers" box under the header, with a pulsing dot beside the title (an exception to "no decorative animation", with the recipe page's folding sections; still under reduced motion; product owner, 2026-11-20), lists the sample offers that apply to the week, those for a planned dish first, each with "Find recipes", which opens the recipe browser filtered to that offer's ingredient (2026-11-20); it is absent when none apply. All buttons are visible and labelled; there is no drag and drop. Weeks saved with Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner slots are shown as dishes on those dates, in the same meal. Every draft dish, picker option, swap, Cook now pick and Home pick uses one rule, `checkEligibility` in `client/src/lib/preference-engine.ts`.

**Store picker** ("Where do you buy your groceries?", 2026-11-20): Groceries → "My stores". Groceries offers it once, the first time the list has items, with "Choose stores" and "Maybe later"; shopping works with no store (2026-11-21; it was the last section of Your household). One row per store: its logo, its name and two independent toggles, Delivery and Walk-in. A tap saves at once; turning off a store's last method removes it. The interface language picks five suggested stores (English and Russian: Walmart, Real Canadian Superstore, No Frills, Safeway, FreshCo; Hebrew: שופרסל, רמי לוי, יוחננוף, ויקטורי, אושר עד); saved stores outside those five stay listed below them. "+ Add another store" takes a name and at least one method; the same name in another capitalization or spacing (or a suggested store's name) reuses that entry. Optional: Groceries work with no store. Any number of stores may be saved; the planned limit of one walk-in shop and two deliveries applies only to a future shopping plan, which is not built.

Supporting features: My Ideas (AI recipe ideas), Import (typed or dictated recipe), My Basic Settings (household, allergies, devices, budget, pantry, Recipe View).

**Recipe View** (Settings, 2026-11-19): Show nutrition (On by default); Nutrition per serving (Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat, Sugars, Fiber, Sodium; Calories, Protein, Fat and Fiber by default), shown while nutrition is on and kept while it is off; Extra recipe details (Prep time, Cook time, Dietary tags, Main cooking tool; all but Dietary tags on by default; defaults changed by the product owner, 2026-11-20, and apply to people who have not saved a Recipe View). A preview with a complete sample recipe updates on every change. One preference, saved with Basic Settings, applies to every recipe card: Home, Recipes, My Ideas, Week → Plan results, the Week's selected recipes and suggested dinners, and Home's examples; the full recipe page adds the nutrition line (it always shows times, tools and tags). Week → Plan's "Show nutrition" switch is the same setting. Recipes, Week → Plan results, My Ideas, Home's examples, the meal generator and the Settings preview use one recipe card (`client/src/components/recipe-card.tsx`, product owner, 2026-11-20): a wide photo (or the labelled example photo) with the like heart on it, the name (opens the recipe), total time and servings, then below a line the selected nutrition as a small grid under "Est. per serving" (name above value), then the selected details; a row with nothing to show is left out. Home's Today uses the same card too (product owner, 2026-11-20): on a phone (under 768px) as above; on wider screens with the photo on the left and the content on its right (`row`). The Week's dishes use its `compact` form at every width on a day with one or two dishes, and the standard form (photo on top) on a day with three or more (2026-11-21): the meal as a label above the card, total time, servings and days on one line, the selected nutrition, prep time, cook time and Dietary tags (product owner, 2026-11-21; the cooking tool stays on the recipe page). Units: kcal, mg for sodium, g otherwise. Dietary tags are no longer shown on cards unless chosen here.

**Home Tasks** (`/home-tasks`, previewed on Home at `/`): add a household task by name in seconds, with suggestions from a seeded catalog, an optional due date, a priority (High, Normal, Low) and one optional photo. Open tasks are grouped Overdue, Today, Upcoming, Anytime; completed tasks are collapsed. Complete, Move to tomorrow and Delete all offer Undo. Home shows the open-task count and, when opened, the open tasks in the same order (changed by the product owner, 2026-11-19). Not in this release: recurring tasks, reminders, assigning to family members, automatic expiry, calendar integration.

## Vocabulary

Use these words in UI copy, code names and docs. Do not introduce synonyms.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The landing page at `/`. Shows Today (dishes cooked or eaten today, by date in the current Week) and a collapsed Home Tasks section with the open-task count. |
| **Home task** | One private household to-do: name, optional due date, priority, optional photo, created and completed times. The nav label is "Home Tasks" ("Tasks" in the phone bottom bar). |
| **Phone bottom bar** | Home, Groceries, Tasks, in that order. Recipes, Week and Ideas are in the phone Menu (product owner, 2026-11-18). |
| **Step ingredients** | The ingredients one cooking step uses, written with that step's quantity ("1 tbsp oil"). Optional recipe data; most recipes have none yet. |
| **Week** | The cooking plan for one calendar week, Monday to Sunday: the dishes cooked on each date (2026-11-20; it had 21 Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner slots before). The nav label is "Week", the page title "Weekly cooking plan"; the route is `/meals` (the nav links to `/meals?plan`, the parameter means nothing; `?week=` names another week by its Monday). |
| **Cooking day** | A date of the Week with at least one dish. Two dishes on Wednesday are one cooking day. |
| **Dish** | One planned batch: a recipe cooked once on a date, with its serving dates and portions. "Make X servings" is its total. Another batch of the same recipe is a separate dish. |
| **Serving date** | A date a dish is eaten, on or after its cooking day (up to the end of the next week), with adults and kids or a direct number of servings. |
| **Catalog** | The shared recipe collection every visitor sees. The UI calls it "Recipes". It is not personal. |
| **Liked recipe** | A catalog recipe the person marked with the heart. Private to its owner. On Recipes, "Liked" and "Imported by me" sit above "All recipes" (product owner, 2026-11-18). |
| **Recipe** | A catalog entry: title, ingredients, steps, times, credit, tags. |
| **Credit** | Who a recipe is attributed to (TableWise, a named source, or AI-generated). Always shown. |
| **Idea** | An AI-generated recipe suggestion in My Ideas, before it is saved to the catalog. |
| **Draft** | A server-side AI generation awaiting the person's approve / regenerate decision. |
| **Cooking session** | One "I made it" record, with optional rating, notes and photos. Private to its owner. |
| **Owner** | The scope of one person's private data. A guest's owner comes from the `tablewise_owner` cookie; an account keeps the owner of the guest who created it. |
| **Account** | An email and password that opens the same owner from any browser, with an optional name and photo. UI words: "Sign in", "Sign out" (not "Login", "Register"). |
| **Guest** | A person using TableWise without signing in. Their data is tied to one browser. |
| **Operator** | The person running the pilot. Signs in at `/operator` with a shared token. Can edit and delete recipes and use YouTube import. |
| **Recipe View** | The Settings section that chooses which nutrition values and extra details recipe cards show. Part of Basic Settings. (Not the older README's name for the recipe page.) |
| **Basic Settings** | Household preferences that personalise planning and ideas. Stored per owner on the server (2026-11-19). |
| **Your household** | The guided setup at `/household`: four questions (cooking style, who you cook for, family food requirements, My kitchen), then Cook now, Plan my week or Personalize my recipes. Its answers are part of Basic Settings. |
| **Personalize my recipes** | The editor for family food requirements, favorite cuisines and optional cooking preferences, opened from screen 5, Cook now, the Week and Settings. Changes wait for Save. |
| **Cook now** | Screen 5 of Your household; opens Find my dish (2026-11-22). Changes nothing. |
| **Find my dish** | Home's search (2026-11-22), opened by Home's "Suggest a recipe" (2026-11-23): opens on a dish; an optional protein, optional Leave out ingredients and dietary needs, then one eligible main dish using that protein as its main ingredient; ingredients tapped as had steer Try another; Start cooking. Changes nothing; never asks for the setup. |
| **Leave out** | An ingredient a Find my dish search must not use, for that search only. (Not an allergy or a dislike, which are saved in Basic Settings.) |
| **Cooking days** | The Week's schedule: evenings per week, fixed evenings and the Mon–Fri limits, asked before the first draft. Part of Basic Settings. |
| **Proteins on hand** | Cook with what I have (2026-11-22): proteins the person has, each with an optional amount. On the Week, "Use selected proteins" stores them (`week_plans.on_hand`), the draft prefers recipes using each one once, and Groceries subtract them. With **Minimize groceries**, recipes adding the fewest ingredient types not already on the grocery list (or in earlier dishes of the week) come first; ties go to a recipe using a protein on hand, then the usual order. |
| **Cooking style** | Quick & easy, Everyday cooking or Chef challenge. Changes the order of suggestions, never what is allowed. |
| **Draft cooking plan** | The cooking plan made once for an unapproved Week: one dish per cooking day. "Approve plan" sends it to Groceries; later edits wait for "Save changes". (Not to be confused with an AI Draft below.) |
| **Weekday limit** | A total-time limit per recipe meal type (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) on Mon–Fri, set with the Week's cooking days; the chosen chef challenge evening's dinner is exempt. Applies to the draft, the dish picker and the Home pickers. Unknown time never passes it. |
| **Pilot event** | One of five funnel events: `planner_opened`, `meal_selected`, `plan_completed`, `grocery_list_opened`, `next_week_plan_created`. |
| **My stores** | The stores a person buys groceries at, each with Delivery, Walk-in or both. Part of Basic Settings. Chosen in the store picker (Groceries → My stores). |
| **Food deals** | Offers shown above the results on Week → Plan, grouped by food category, and as "Weekly deals · N offers" on the Week. Today they are hardcoded and labelled "Sample deals". |
| **MVP planner / MVP grocery** | The earlier three-dinner flow (`mvp-planner.tsx`, `/grocery-list?source=mvp`). Legacy; do not extend. |

## Product principles

1. **Calm.** Minimal interface, generous whitespace, no gamification, no streaks, no decorative animation. The interface should disappear while cooking.
2. **Works without AI.** Planning, groceries and cooking must work fully with AI switched off. AI is an addition, never a dependency.
3. **Honest about content.** Every recipe shows its credit. AI-generated and estimated data are labelled as such.
4. **Private by default.** Cooking history, notes and photos are visible only to their owner.
5. **Plain language.** End users never see operator, developer or infrastructure wording ("operator access", "paid AI", "router").
6. **Three languages equally.** English, Russian and Hebrew (right-to-left). No hardcoded English strings.
7. **Phone first.** 44px touch targets, bottom tab bar (Home, Groceries, Tasks), safe-area padding.

## Out of scope for the pilot

Do not build these unless the product owner asks: payments or plans, recipe sharing or public recipe pages, SEO, email or push notifications, social features, scraping recipes from links, native apps.

## Known gaps

These are accepted facts today, not features. Do not build on top of them as if they work.

- **Weeks are dated, grocery progress is not.** Each week is its own plan (2026-11-19); plans saved earlier became the week the change shipped in. Plans saved with Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner slots (before 2026-11-20) are read as dishes cooked on those dates, their servings split evenly over spanned days; they do not know which day was cooking and which was leftovers. Checked grocery items and purchases are kept per browser, not per week. The grocery screen's own pilot event still uses the calendar week it happens in.
- **Accounts are minimal.** Email and password only: no password reset (there is no email), no email verification, no Google sign-in yet. A forgotten password loses the account. Grocery progress and My Ideas stay in browser storage and do not follow the account; Basic Settings do (2026-11-19). Signing in on a browser that already has guest data leaves that data with the guest; it is not merged. A guest's data is still tied to one browser.
- **Ideas live in browser storage only.** The Week (2026-11-18; per owner and week since 2026-11-19) and Basic Settings (2026-11-19) are stored in Postgres per owner. New per-user data must go in Postgres (see ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Photo and link import do not parse anything.** A photo is attached as an image; a link is stored as the source URL. Only typed and dictated text is turned into a recipe.
- **Several Week → Plan filters have no catalog data yet** and match nothing until recipes are tagged: Cooking level, Keto, Low-sodium, Low added sugar, Egg-free, Peanut-free, Tree-nut-free, Soy-free, Kosher, Halal. High-protein, Low-carb, Lower-calorie and High-fibre match only the few recipes with nutrition data. The earlier filters were restored on 2026-11-19, but four have no data and always show no recipes: My picks and Minimum rating (nothing records a pick or rating any more; the heart is "Liked"), With my notes and Modified by me (never recorded). Source "Trusted authors" and "Personal" also match nothing; only "My recipes" and "Imported" do.
- **Recipe View nutrition is thin.** Only recipes with an AI nutrition estimate and a single serving count (not a range such as "4–6" or a yield such as "12 muffins") show values, always labelled "Est. per serving". Sodium is never analysed, so it shows nowhere except the Settings preview. Nothing stores per-serving values yet.
- **The store catalog is mock data.** Ten stores with bundled logos (`client/src/lib/grocery-stores.ts`, `client/public/store-logos/`, from Wikimedia); the language, not the person's location, picks the suggestions. Stores are saved; only Weekly deals on the Week use them (to filter the sample offers). No prices or shopping plan.
- **Grocery prices are sample data.** An item with no saved price gets a made-up CAD price from its text (CA$1.50–24.99, the same every time, not scaled by any real unit price), labelled "Sample prices in CAD, not from a store"; nothing saves prices since "Set estimated price" was removed (2026-11-18). Prices show in CA$ in every language.
- **Food deals are sample data.** There is no deals source; added categories last for the visit only. Weekly deals on the Week show only offers from My stores when any are chosen (all sample offers when none are), and the sample offers have no end date, so they show every week.
- **Weeks run Monday to Sunday.** A Sunday dish eaten on Tuesday serves the next week, and Move day stays within the shown week. Home shows dishes eaten today only from the current week's plan, so a Sunday dish eaten on Monday does not appear on Monday's Home.
- **Anonymous visitors can add AI-generated recipes to the shared catalog** without review when public AI is on.
- **Any signed-in account can add a typed or pasted recipe to the shared catalog** without review (opened 2026-11-17; guests see the import page with saving disabled and a note to sign in, product owner, 2026-11-18). Only the operator can remove it.
- **"Imported by me" starts on 2026-11-18.** Recipes imported earlier have no recorded importer and appear only under "All recipes".
- **Catalog recipes have no images**, and only a few have nutrition data. On Recipes and the recipe page, a recipe without a photo shows one of three CC0 example photos of its main protein, labelled "Example photo" (2026-11-20; `client/src/lib/recipe-photo.ts`). The protein is guessed from English ingredient words, so a recipe in another language shows a vegetable photo; Home, Week and My Ideas thumbnails do not use them.
- **Recipe translations are partial (2026-11-20).** A recipe with a row in `recipe_translations` for the reader's language shows that title on every recipe card and that title, ingredients and steps on the recipe page and in cooking mode, with "Translated by AI · Show original". Grocery lists, Home's meal names, search and the safety checks still read the original text. Nothing writes translations yet: import and AI generation do not, and the 20 local test recipes were translated by hand-run backfill (`script/translate-recipes.ts`, which needs a working OpenAI key). A translation whose line count no longer matches the recipe is not shown.
- **Only the 20 book recipes and the three Chef Prati recipes have step ingredients (2026-11-23), in the local and production databases.** For the book recipes, `data/catalog/gf-book-v1-step-ingredients.json` (loaded by `gf-book-v1-step-ingredients.sql`) lists, for each step, the ingredients it names with that step's amount (half the salt, 2 tbsp of the oil), written by hand from the steps; an ingredient handled in several steps is listed in each. They are written in English; in Russian and Hebrew Zen mode shows the matching translated ingredient line, scaled for a part (½ of "1 onion"), so the unit word can keep the full amount's form ("1 כפות"); a line matching no ingredient stays in English (`shownStepIngredients` in `client/src/lib/ingredient-scale.ts`). Import and AI generation do not write step ingredients and there is no editor, so every other recipe shows "View recipe ingredients".
- **Kid-friendly pairs are not built (2026-11-21).** The handoff asks for one complete kid-friendly dish or two complementary ones on one meal card. The catalog marks neither "complete dish" nor "side", so only single dishes are suggested; pairs wait for reviewed main/side data and grouped meal cards on the Week. 98 of the 200 catalog recipes are tagged Kid friendly (33 dinners, 14 lunches); the local database has 4 of 20.
- **Kitchen equipment is read from the steps (2026-11-21).** Steps that name no device are missing data, not "no-cook": such a recipe is offered only to a kitchen with all four appliances. "No cooking appliances" matches only recipes reviewed as no-cook: 11 of the 200 catalog recipes (`client/src/lib/verified-no-cook.ts`, with the reason for each), none of them a dinner, and none in the 20-recipe local database. No catalog recipe can be made with only a microwave, so a microwave-only kitchen gets the reviewed no-cook recipes only. A kitchen never answered (`devices: null`) counts as no appliance until the question is answered, except in Find my dish, where it filters nothing.
- **Paleo and Carnivore rest on 10 demo recipes (2026-11-21).** Both diets need the explicit `Paleo` or `Carnivore` tag and pass conservative ingredient rules (`shared/food-rules.ts`: Paleo has no grains, legumes, dairy or refined sugar; Carnivore only meat, fish, seafood, eggs, animal fats, plain dairy, water and salt). The only tagged recipes are five AI-generated ones per diet (`script/diet-recipes.ts`, `data/catalog/diet-recipes-v1.sql`, ids `tablewise:diet:v1:*`, labelled AI-generated), in the local and production databases. Paleo has two Asian dishes (Thai, Korean); Carnivore has none. The existing catalog has not been reviewed for either diet, so none of its recipes qualify yet.
- **Suggestions rely on thin catalog data.** Only Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Paleo and Carnivore are explicit catalog tags; a strict diet or the Gluten or Milk allergy needs that tag and clean ingredient wording, so untagged recipes (imports, AI recipes) are never suggested to those households. Other allergies (eggs, nuts, soy, fish, shellfish, sesame, mustard) rely on ingredient wording only; Your household offers them with that caveat. There is no difficulty data: "how involved" is estimated from steps, ingredient lines and total time, for ranking only. Not built for lack of data: hands-on time, technique, pantry use, familiar-versus-new, cooking extra portions. With a strict restriction the catalog has few dinners (about 6 vegan, 38–46 dairy- or gluten-free).
- **Sign in has no separate "Create account" button.** The request for Create account / Sign in / Continue as guest is met by the existing screen: the first sign-in with a new email creates the account.
- **The meal generator only searches the catalog.** Free search matches English words in recipe titles, ingredients and tags; other words (and other languages) find nothing and say so. It never calls AI or creates a recipe. It does not show nutrition.
- **Cook with what I have rests on thin data (2026-11-22).** "Bought last week" is read from the browser's grocery progress, which is not per week: any recorded purchase of a protein counts, from this browser only. No purchase store is recorded anywhere, so none is shown. Proteins and ingredient types are matched by English name words ("cooked rice" and "rice" are two types; "chicken" matches "chicken thighs"). Only g/kg and ml/L convert: lb, oz, cups and counts against grams are asked about, never guessed. Must include is no longer offered by any button (only the old Cook now page reads it from a link). Photo, video and AI identification are not built.
- **Find my dish rests on thin data (2026-11-22).** The main ingredient is the first needed ingredient line naming a protein (stocks, sauces and spices skipped); nothing in the catalog marks it, nor a main dish or a side, so a lunch or dinner counts as a main dish. Proteins are matched by English words: the dropdown's choices work in every language, but own text in Russian or Hebrew finds nothing. Production's coverage is not checked. Start cooking shows step ingredients only for the 20 book and three Chef Prati recipes; others show "View recipe ingredients".
