# TableWise — Product Definition

> Source of truth for what the product is. If code or another doc disagrees with this file, raise it; do not silently pick one.
> Last reviewed: 2026-11-20.

## What it is

TableWise is a weekly meal planner for households. A person picks dinners for the week, gets one grocery list, cooks with a step-by-step mode, and keeps a private record of what they made. Home Tasks, a short private to-do list for the house, sits beside the meal loop (added by the product owner, 2026-11-17).

It is **not** a recipe social network, a recipe scraper, or a general AI chat.

## Who it is for

A person who plans and cooks for a household: several eaters, allergies and dislikes, a fixed set of kitchen devices, a budget, and limited time on weeknights.

## Stage and hypothesis

- **Stage:** gated pilot. Optional email and password accounts (guests need none), no payments, no marketing site.
- **Hypothesis under test:** people find the plan → groceries → cook loop valuable enough to come back and plan another week.
- **Measure of success:** `next_week_plan_created` following a `plan_completed` for the same person.

## Core loop

1. **Plan** (`/meals`) — the Week as a cooking plan: one dated week (Monday–Sunday) grouped by cooking day, each dish shown once on the day it is cooked with how many servings to make and the dates it serves, opened as a draft and approved in one action (product owner, 2026-11-20, replacing the 21 Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner slots of 2026-11-19; see Cooking plan below). Screen: [docs/cooking-plan.md](cooking-plan.md). Previous and next week controls open other weeks. "Browse recipes" (`/meals?browse`) searches and filters the catalog and keeps up to 12 picks. The recipe browser is the only place to set recipe filters: Cooking level, Total time (Any or 15–60 min, ≤30 min by default), Dietary (with More dietary options), Kid favourites, Kitchen tools (your devices and No-cook), Source, and More filters (protein, meal type, must include, must exclude, cuisine, My picks with minimum rating, With my notes, Modified by me; restored by the product owner, 2026-11-19). Food deals above the results shows sample offers by food category; Show recipes filters to recipes using that ingredient (product owner, 2026-11-18). Rules: [docs/week-plan-filters.md](week-plan-filters.md).
2. **Shop** (`/grocery-list`) — one list built from the approved dishes of the Week, each counted once and scaled from its recipe's original yield to its "Make X servings" (a draft dish, or a recipe only picked in the browser, adds nothing until Approve plan or Save changes; 2026-11-20), grouped by category, with pantry items marked. Share or export it. Add household products using translated suggestions or a custom name, with an optional private reference picture. Add products individually or paste a list, review estimated category costs (each category's "$"), and record full or partial purchases. Under the header, "Walk-in estimate" is the sum of every remaining item's price, whatever the filter or search shows, and "Find an item…" searches every category, collapsed ones too (2026-11-20). Removing a main protein can also remove its dishes from the Week. Check the impact before confirming and use Undo to restore removed dishes. Purchased quantities stay available under Checked items.
3. **Cook** (`/recipe/:id`) — scale servings, check off ingredients, run cooking mode with per-step timers. Finishing cooking mode returns to the recipe with "Made it?" at the top of the screen and "I made it! ✓" focused (2026-11-21). Start cooking opens Zen mode at step 1 by default. In Zen mode, the ingredients the current step uses (with that step's quantities) are listed above its instruction, and they change with Next and Back. A step that uses none shows no ingredient section. A recipe without step-level ingredient data shows "View recipe ingredients" (the full list) instead; ingredients are never guessed (product owner, 2026-11-18).
4. **Remember** (My Cooking Experience on the recipe page) — "I made it", rating, notes, photos.

**Home** (`/`) is the landing page: Today from the Week and Home Tasks, nothing else. Today is split into Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner, as in the Week (product owner, 2026-11-20); each meal lists the dishes cooked today ("Make X servings") and dishes cooked earlier that are eaten today ("Cooked Sun"), each opening the recipe at that batch and with "Remove dish" (the whole dish, as on the Week, with Undo; 2026-11-20), and its own "+ Add breakfast", "+ Add lunch" or "+ Add dinner". A meal with nothing today is collapsed to its name; opening it shows its "+ Add" (2026-11-20). An earlier meal of the day that has dishes is collapsed too, and its header shows each dish on one line: the name (cut short if long), the first two Recipe View nutrients and an underlined "…" that shows all of them below (as on the recipe card, smaller and without the "Est. per serving" heading); opening it shows the cards instead (2026-11-20). Home Tasks starts collapsed, with the number of open tasks beside its title; opening it lists every open task and "View all tasks". Today's dishes are full recipe cards (on wider screens with the photo on the left), so Home can scroll down (product owner, 2026-11-20; it did not scroll before). "+ Add breakfast/lunch/dinner" opens the meal generator (a bottom sheet on a phone, a dialog on desktop; product owner, 2026-11-19): the kind of dish to search (Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner; that meal first, and the dish is saved under the kind chosen), an optional free search ("What would you like for dinner?"), Time (Any time, ≤ 15 min, ≤ 30 min of total time; unknown time never passes), Cooking style (Quick & easy by default) and the household line (servings, restrictions, budget) with Edit for servings, the four marked diets and kitchen equipment, for this dish only (equipment added 2026-11-20). "Generate dinner" shows one catalog recipe labelled "Suggested recipe" with time, servings, scaled ingredients and steps; "Try another", "Edit request" and "Choose from my recipes" (Liked and Imported by me) keep the inputs. Only "Add to today" changes the Week: a new dish cooked today, beside any already there, at the household's adults and kids (or the servings edited in the sheet). Home then shows "Added to today" with Undo, which removes only that dish and only while it is still that recipe on that date. Allergies, devices (as edited for this dish) and meal rules always apply through `checkEligibility`; Week → Plan filters are not applied. No AI is used (`client/src/lib/meal-generator.ts`).

**Sign in** (first visit, at any page except a share link): email and password, or "Continue as guest". There is no separate sign-up: the first sign-in with a new email creates the account with the password typed, and the guest's data becomes the account's. Basic Settings shows who is signed in, with Sign out (product owner, 2026-11-18). A signed-in account can add an optional name and photo there; the photo (or the first letter of the name or email) replaces the Settings icon in the header (2026-11-18).

**Your household** (`/household`, 2026-11-19): after Sign in or "Continue as guest", a first-time person (no saved settings and an empty Week) answers one screen: cooking style (Quick & easy, Everyday cooking, Chef challenge), servings, dietary requirements (only Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free and Dairy-free, the ones the catalog marks explicitly) and allergies (all ten from Basic Settings; Gluten and Milk also need the catalog mark, the other eight are checked by ingredient wording and unclear wording counts as unsafe; product owner, 2026-11-19); nothing is pre-selected, so the person must pick a diet or allergy or choose "None" explicitly before "Suggest my week", kitchen equipment (required, nothing pre-selected; product owner, 2026-11-19), cooking evenings per week (1–7 or "Not sure yet" = three, or more if more fixed days), and optional fixed evenings. Then a weekday (Mon–Fri) total-time limit for each meal, e.g. 10 minutes for breakfast and 40 for dinner (product owner, 2026-11-19), for Chef challenge when the challenge happens, and the Week → Plan recipe filters: the same panel and the same saved state as the Week page, so a filter chosen on either page shows on the other (product owner, 2026-11-19). In Your household the panel leaves out Dietary and Kitchen tools (already asked above), puts Meal type first and shows Cuisine up front as optional. Suggestions, swaps and replacements apply those filters after the restrictions. Every question is a collapsible group whose header shows its answer in small text; choosing something in a group collapses the groups above it. A "None" answer to restrictions folds that group, also on return, with "+ Add an allergy or diet" under it to reopen it (2026-11-20). Quick & easy starts with the weekday limits and filters collapsed, Everyday cooking with the filters collapsed, Chef challenge with everything open. "Suggest my week" puts one catalog dish (no AI) on each cooking day and opens the Week (2026-11-20); "Skip for now" goes Home. Returning people and anyone with a Week skip it; it stays reachable from the Week and from the TableWise name in the header (product owner, 2026-11-19). A Week that already has dishes is not changed by it: when answers that change dishes (Your household or Basic Settings, not name, stores or Recipe View) were saved after the Week was planned, the Week asks once "Your preferences have been changed": "Update the week" replaces this week's dishes with a new draft (an approved Week keeps its groceries until Save changes), "Keep my week" leaves it (product owner, 2026-11-20).

**Cooking plan** (`/meals?plan`, "Weekly cooking plan", product owner 2026-11-20; full rules in [docs/cooking-plan.md](cooking-plan.md)): seven dated days, Monday to Sunday, today highlighted, grouped by cooking day (redesigned the same day). A day can hold any number of dishes; two dishes on Wednesday are one cooking day. A cooking day lists its dishes in Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner order, with "Move day" beside the date and one "+ Add dish" below, which asks Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner and opens that meal's picker (its recipes first); a day without cooking is one line, the date and "+ Add dish". Each dish appears once, on the day it is cooked, as a compact card: the recipe name (opens the recipe at this batch; Back returns to the same week), then one line "Dinner · 45 min · 7 servings · For Mon–Tue" (total time only; the servings label opens Edit servings; the days covered are left out when the dish is eaten only on its cooking day, "Mon–Wed" for consecutive days, "Mon & Wed" for separate ones), then two icon buttons, Swap (arrows) and Remove dish (bin), labelled for screen readers (product owner, 2026-11-20). A dish keeps its meal through Swap, Edit servings and moves; dishes saved without one, and the draft's, are dinners. Servings for a date are adults + kids × 0.5 (from the household members; a fixed number counts as adults; with neither, the recipe's own yield), or a direct amount in half servings; Make X servings is their sum, and ingredients scale from the recipe's original yield (asked for when the recipe has none). Edit servings picks any dates from the cooking day to the end of next week, per date adults and kids or a direct amount, with a live total; at least one date, a positive total, no date before the cooking day. Swap keeps the dates and portions. Move day moves all dishes of that day together within the week; portions that would come before the new day are listed and must each be moved to the new day or removed before Move; Cancel changes nothing. The draft is made once per week: every preferred weekday from Your household is a cooking day, then days are added until the weekly count is met, each the farthest from the nearest cooking day (Sunday and Monday adjacent, ties to the earlier day), one eligible dish each. Reloads keep every edit. "Approve plan", in the header beside the status with "Approval builds your grocery list.", copies the dishes to Groceries; afterwards the header shows "View groceries", and after any edit "Save changes". An always-open "Weekly deals · N offers" box under the header, with a pulsing dot beside the title (the one exception to "no decorative animation"; still under reduced motion; product owner, 2026-11-20), lists the sample offers that apply to the week, those for a planned dish first, each with "Find recipes", which opens the recipe browser filtered to that offer's ingredient (2026-11-20); it is absent when none apply. All buttons are visible and labelled; there is no drag and drop. Weeks saved with Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner slots are shown as dishes on those dates, in the same meal. Home → Today → "Suggest a recipe" shows one eligible dinner for today with "Start cooking" (Zen mode) and "Suggest another"; it changes neither the Week nor Groceries. Every draft dish, picker option, swap and Home pick uses one rule, `checkEligibility` in `client/src/lib/preference-engine.ts`.

**Store picker** ("Where do you buy your groceries?", 2026-11-20): the last section of Your household and Groceries → "My stores". One row per store: its logo, its name and two independent toggles, Delivery and Walk-in. A tap saves at once; turning off a store's last method removes it. The interface language picks five suggested stores (English and Russian: Walmart, Real Canadian Superstore, No Frills, Safeway, FreshCo; Hebrew: שופרסל, רמי לוי, יוחננוף, ויקטורי, אושר עד); saved stores outside those five stay listed below them. "+ Add another store" takes a name and at least one method; the same name in another capitalization or spacing (or a suggested store's name) reuses that entry. Optional: Your household continues with no store. Any number of stores may be saved; the planned limit of one walk-in shop and two deliveries applies only to a future shopping plan, which is not built.

Supporting features: My Ideas (AI recipe ideas), Import (typed or dictated recipe), My Basic Settings (household, allergies, devices, budget, pantry, Recipe View).

**Recipe View** (Settings, 2026-11-19): Show nutrition (On by default); Nutrition per serving (Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat, Sugars, Fiber, Sodium; Calories, Protein, Fat and Fiber by default), shown while nutrition is on and kept while it is off; Extra recipe details (Prep time, Cook time, Dietary tags, Main cooking tool; all but Dietary tags on by default; defaults changed by the product owner, 2026-11-20, and apply to people who have not saved a Recipe View). A preview with a complete sample recipe updates on every change. One preference, saved with Basic Settings, applies to every recipe card: Home, Recipes, My Ideas, Week → Plan results, the Week's selected recipes and suggested dinners, and Home's suggestion; the full recipe page adds the nutrition line (it always shows times, tools and tags). Week → Plan's "Show nutrition" switch is the same setting. Recipes, Week → Plan results, My Ideas, Home's suggestion, the meal generator and the Settings preview use one recipe card (`client/src/components/recipe-card.tsx`, product owner, 2026-11-20): a wide photo (or the labelled example photo) with the like heart on it, the name (opens the recipe), total time and servings, then below a line the selected nutrition as a small grid under "Est. per serving" (name above value), then the selected details; a row with nothing to show is left out. Home's Today uses the same card too (product owner, 2026-11-20): on a phone (under 768px) as above; on wider screens with the photo on the left and the content on its right (`row`). The Week's dishes use its `compact` form at every width: a small photo on the left, the meal, total time, servings and days on one line, the selected nutrition and Dietary tags (prep time, cook time and the cooking tool stay on the recipe page). Units: kcal, mg for sodium, g otherwise. Dietary tags are no longer shown on cards unless chosen here.

**Home Tasks** (`/home-tasks`, previewed on Home at `/`): add a household task by name in seconds, with suggestions from a seeded catalog, an optional due date, a priority (High, Normal, Low) and one optional photo. Open tasks are grouped Overdue, Today, Upcoming, Anytime; completed tasks are collapsed. Complete, Move to tomorrow and Delete all offer Undo. Home shows the open-task count and, when opened, the open tasks in the same order (changed by the product owner, 2026-11-19). Not in this release: recurring tasks, reminders, assigning to family members, automatic expiry, calendar integration.

## Product principles

1. **Calm.** Minimal interface, generous whitespace, no gamification, no streaks, no decorative animation. The interface should disappear while cooking.
2. **Works without AI.** Planning, groceries and cooking must work fully with AI switched off. AI is an addition, never a dependency.
3. **Honest about content.** Every recipe shows its credit. AI-generated and estimated data are labelled as such.
4. **Private by default.** Cooking history, notes and photos are visible only to their owner.
5. **Plain language.** End users never see operator, developer or infrastructure wording ("operator access", "paid AI", "router").
6. **Three languages equally.** English, Russian and Hebrew (right-to-left). No hardcoded English strings.
7. **Phone first.** 44px touch targets, bottom tab bar (Home, Groceries, Tasks), safe-area padding.

## Out of scope for the pilot

Do not build these unless the product owner asks: payments or plans, recipe sharing or public recipe pages, SEO, email or push notifications, social features, scraping recipes from links, native apps.

## Known gaps

These are accepted facts today, not features. Do not build on top of them as if they work.

- **Weeks are dated, grocery progress is not.** Each week is its own plan (2026-11-19); plans saved earlier became the week the change shipped in. Plans saved with Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner slots (before 2026-11-20) are read as dishes cooked on those dates, their servings split evenly over spanned days; they do not know which day was cooking and which was leftovers. Checked grocery items and purchases are kept per browser, not per week. The grocery screen's own pilot event still uses the calendar week it happens in.
- **Accounts are minimal.** Email and password only: no password reset (there is no email), no email verification, no Google sign-in yet. A forgotten password loses the account. Grocery progress and My Ideas stay in browser storage and do not follow the account; Basic Settings do (2026-11-19). Signing in on a browser that already has guest data leaves that data with the guest; it is not merged. A guest's data is still tied to one browser.
- **Ideas live in browser storage only.** The Week (2026-11-18; per owner and week since 2026-11-19) and Basic Settings (2026-11-19) are stored in Postgres per owner. New per-user data must go in Postgres (see ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Photo and link import do not parse anything.** A photo is attached as an image; a link is stored as the source URL. Only typed and dictated text is turned into a recipe.
- **Several Week → Plan filters have no catalog data yet** and match nothing until recipes are tagged: Cooking level, Keto, Paleo, Low-sodium, Low added sugar, Egg-free, Peanut-free, Tree-nut-free, Soy-free, Kosher, Halal. High-protein, Low-carb, Lower-calorie and High-fibre match only the few recipes with nutrition data. The earlier filters were restored on 2026-11-19, but four have no data and always show no recipes: My picks and Minimum rating (nothing records a pick or rating any more; the heart is "Liked"), With my notes and Modified by me (never recorded). Source "Trusted authors" and "Personal" also match nothing; only "My recipes" and "Imported" do.
- **Recipe View nutrition is thin.** Only recipes with an AI nutrition estimate and a single serving count (not a range such as "4–6" or a yield such as "12 muffins") show values, always labelled "Est. per serving". Sodium is never analysed, so it shows nowhere except the Settings preview. Nothing stores per-serving values yet.
- **The store catalog is mock data.** Ten stores with bundled logos (`client/src/lib/grocery-stores.ts`, `client/public/store-logos/`, from Wikimedia); the language, not the person's location, picks the suggestions. Stores are saved; only Weekly deals on the Week use them (to filter the sample offers). No prices or shopping plan.
- **Grocery prices are sample data.** An item with no saved price gets a made-up CAD price from its text (CA$1.50–24.99, the same every time, not scaled by any real unit price), labelled "Sample prices in CAD, not from a store"; nothing saves prices since "Set estimated price" was removed (2026-11-18). Prices show in CA$ in every language.
- **Food deals are sample data.** There is no deals source; added categories last for the visit only. Weekly deals on the Week show only offers from My stores when any are chosen (all sample offers when none are), and the sample offers have no end date, so they show every week.
- **Weeks run Monday to Sunday.** A Sunday dish eaten on Tuesday serves the next week, and Move day stays within the shown week. Home shows dishes eaten today only from the current week's plan, so a Sunday dish eaten on Monday does not appear on Monday's Home.
- **Anonymous visitors can add AI-generated recipes to the shared catalog** without review when public AI is on.
- **Any signed-in account can add a typed or pasted recipe to the shared catalog** without review (opened 2026-11-17; guests see the import page with saving disabled and a note to sign in, product owner, 2026-11-18). Only the operator can remove it.
- **"Imported by me" starts on 2026-11-18.** Recipes imported earlier have no recorded importer and appear only under "All recipes".
- **Catalog recipes have no images**, and only a few have nutrition data. On Recipes and the recipe page, a recipe without a photo shows one of three CC0 example photos of its main protein, labelled "Example photo" (2026-11-20; `client/src/lib/recipe-photo.ts`). The protein is guessed from English ingredient words, so a recipe in another language shows a vegetable photo; Home, Week and My Ideas thumbnails do not use them.
- **Recipe translations are partial (2026-11-20).** A recipe with a row in `recipe_translations` for the reader's language shows that title on every recipe card and that title, ingredients and steps on the recipe page and in cooking mode, with "Translated by AI · Show original". Grocery lists, Home's meal names, search and the safety checks still read the original text. Nothing writes translations yet: import and AI generation do not, and the 20 local test recipes were translated by hand-run backfill (`script/translate-recipes.ts`, which needs a working OpenAI key). A translation whose line count no longer matches the recipe is not shown.
- **No recipe has step ingredients yet.** The data field exists (2026-11-18), but nothing fills it: import and AI generation do not write it, and there is no editor. Until it is filled, Zen mode shows "View recipe ingredients" for every recipe.
- **Suggestions rely on thin catalog data.** Only Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free and Dairy-free are explicit catalog tags; a strict diet or the Gluten or Milk allergy needs that tag and clean ingredient wording, so untagged recipes (imports, AI recipes) are never suggested to those households. Other allergies (eggs, nuts, soy, fish, shellfish, sesame, mustard) rely on ingredient wording only; Your household offers them with that caveat. There is no difficulty data: "how involved" is estimated from steps, ingredient lines and total time, for ranking only. Not built for lack of data: hands-on time, technique, pantry use, familiar-versus-new, cooking extra portions. With a strict restriction the catalog has few dinners (about 6 vegan, 38–46 dairy- or gluten-free).
- **Sign in has no separate "Create account" button.** The request for Create account / Sign in / Continue as guest is met by the existing screen: the first sign-in with a new email creates the account.
- **The meal generator only searches the catalog.** Free search matches English words in recipe titles, ingredients and tags; other words (and other languages) find nothing and say so. It never calls AI or creates a recipe. It does not show nutrition.
- **README.md and replit.md describe an older product** ("recipe manager from photos, links and voice", "Recipe View"). This file supersedes them.
